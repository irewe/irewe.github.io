## Introduction
On this website I gather some of my published and unpublished work.

I am currently a PhD student at the Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences (SLU) and employed by the Swedish Veterinary Agency (SVA). 

In my PhD-project "MycoModel" we develop a disease spread model for *Mycoplasma bovis* (a bacterium that affects cattle) and aim to enhance the epidemiological knowledge and to stop the spread. [Read more here](/projects/mycomodel/)

My background is a masters degree in engineering (environment/water). However, a big interest in data, math and animals led me to veterinary epidemiologi.
